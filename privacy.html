Privacy notice

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Short version

Luce has no accounts and no tracking. Your movie lists, ratings, notes and settings stay in your browser (localStorage). We never see them, and we never collect your email.

Data controller

This is a personal, non-commercial project by Simone Loru. Contact via the GitHub repository issues page: github.com/simoneloru/luci.

What we store (nothing on our servers)

  • Your data (watched movies, watchlist, ratings 1–5, notes, watch dates, UI language) is stored only in your browser's localStorage under keys like luci.v1 and luci.lang. It never leaves your device except when you explicitly use Export (you download a JSON file).
  • Your TMDB API key, if you enter one in Settings, is stored only in your browser (luci.tmdbKey). Never commit it to the repo.
  • We do not collect or store email addresses. Email would only be needed if accounts are introduced in the future — and that would require your explicit consent plus an updated notice.

Third parties

  • Movie data: search terms and movie lookups are sent over HTTPS to The Movie Database (TMDB) API to show titles, posters, cast and runtimes. See TMDB privacy policy.
  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages, which may process technical logs (e.g. IP address) to operate the service. See GitHub privacy statement.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no profiling cookies. Only technically necessary browser storage is used.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, processing is limited to what is strictly necessary to provide the site you requested (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and to our legitimate interest in running a secure, functional site (Art. 6(1)(f)). No marketing processing takes place.

Your rights

You can access and delete everything at any time: your data lives in your browser, so clearing site data (or removing entries via the app / Export file) erases it. To exercise GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection), contact us via the repository page and we will reply.

Retention

Local data is kept until you delete it. Exported JSON files are yours: keep or delete them as you like. Server logs (GitHub/TMDB) follow those providers' retention policies.

About saving an email in the future

Saving an email is allowed, but it is personal data: it needs a clear purpose, a legal basis (usually consent), data minimization (email only, nothing more), EU-region storage, and the ability to export/delete the account. That is why the current version deliberately avoids accounts.

Changes

If the app changes (e.g. accounts are added), this notice will be updated and the update date revised.
